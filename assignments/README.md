My Assignments

A personal showcase page that brings together my BCA Semester I assignments in one place. It covers my work across web development, visual design and structured problem-solving, from static layouts to interactive builds.

Live page: https://rajatsharma31.github.io/My-Assignments/

What the page contains
A sidebar with my name, the page title and a short introduction. It stays in view while scrolling.
A grid of 12 assignment cards. Each card has a screenshot of the project, a number, a title and a short description of what the brief asked and what I made.
Hover effects on every card: it lifts up, the border highlights, the shadow deepens, the screenshot zooms slightly and an "Open the project" link appears.
A dark theme with a consistent blue and gold colour palette.
A responsive layout that adapts to laptops, tablets and phones.
